# F9: 持仓时长与管理频率实证

## Findings

### [1] FinMem 将长期记忆按信息时效分三层，重要性衰减到阈值 5 所需时间分别为 30 / 90 / 365 天（shallow / intermediate / deep）
- quote: "Through experimentation, we set α_shallow=0.9, α_intermediate=0.967 and α_deep=0.988. This ensures that θ_l decreases to a threshold score of 5 after intervals of 30, 90, and 365 days for shallow, intermediate, and deep layers, respectively."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13743v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-12-03 (v2)
- confidence: high

### [2] FinMem 工作记忆容量 = 3×K（K 为每层检索的 top-K 事件），消融实验测过 K = 1, 3, 5, 10；人类工作记忆上限为 7±2
- quote: "The working memory capacity is thus set at 3×K, mirroring the human cognitive system's limit of processing immediate messages upon specific stimuli (3 refers to the three processing layers in long-term memory). ... We tested K values of 1, 3, 5, and 10, exploring FinMem's working memory capabilities at levels below, near, and above the human cognitive limit."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13743v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-12-03 (v2)
- confidence: high

### [3] FinMem 扩展反思（extended reflection）覆盖 M 天轨迹窗口；K 与 M 是工作记忆容量/检索能力的两个超参，均针对「每日一次」的交易询问设计
- quote: "Extended reflection reevaluates immediate reflection outcomes for a ticker over a specified M-day trace period. ... K and M are hyperparameters to adjust FinMem's working memory capacity and information retrieval ability."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13743v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-12-03 (v2)
- confidence: high

### [4] FinMem 用 6–12 个月的每日数据训练即可获得稳健表现；决策节奏是「每日交易询问」（daily trading inquiry）
- quote: "Our experiments indicate that training FinMem with daily collected data over just six months to a year is enough to produce robust and notable trading results. ... Immediate reflection is activated upon receiving a daily trading inquiry for a specific ticker."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13743v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-12-03 (v2)
- confidence: high

### [5] FinCon 每个 agent 检索 top-5 记忆事件进入决策上下文；重要性按 Ebbinghaus 遗忘曲线指数衰减，关键事件访问计数 +5 加权
- quote: "we set the number of top memory events retrieved for each agent at 5. ... S_Importance = v^E × θ^δt ... A memory ID deemed critical to investment gains receives +5 to its importance score."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.06567v3
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11-07 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [6] FinCon 决策与信念更新均为「日频」：POMDP 以日 PnL 为奖励，自我反思在 CVaR 下降或当日 PnL<0 时触发
- quote: "the reward function uses daily profit & loss (PnL) as the output; ... the reflection process represents the manager agent's self-reflection, which is updated from B_t to B_{t+1} on daily basis. ... if ρ_t < ρ_{t-1} or r_t < 0 then Trigger M_a self-reflection"
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.06567v3
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11-07 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [7] FinCon 训练窗口选择覆盖至少一个 10-Q / 电话会议 / 10-K 披露周期（约一个季度），使记忆保留期与财报节奏对齐
- quote: "The training period was chosen to account for the seasonal nature of corporate financial reporting and the duration of data retention in FinCon's memory module. The selected training duration ensures the inclusion of at least one publication cycle of either Form 10-Q, ECC, or Form 10-K."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.06567v3
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11-07 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [8] FinCon 的程序性记忆检索分数 γ = 相关性 + 重要性（余弦相似 + 时间衰减），与 Park et al. 生成式 agent 的 recency/relevancy/importance 三因子同源但合并了 recency
- quote: "γ^E = S_Relevancy^E + S_Importance^E ... adapted from Park et al. but with modified relevancy and importance computations ... in our design, the factors of recency and importance are handled by one equation."
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.06567v3
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11-07 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [9] FinAgent（FinMem 后继）采用双层反思 + 多样化记忆检索，在 6 个含加密货币的数据集上平均利润提升 36%；论文以「日频」资产决策评估
- quote: "Its unique dual-level reflection module not only enables rapid adaptation to market dynamics but also incorporates a diversified memory retrieval system... FinAgent significantly outperforms 9 state-of-the-art baselines in terms of 6 financial metrics with over 36% average improvement on profit. ... on 6 financial datasets, including stocks and Crypto"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18485
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-28 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [10] TradingAgents 采用日频 buy/sell/hold 信号（2024-01-01 至 2024-03-29 回测），交易员综合「辩论 + 历史数据」，但论文未给出显式的历史决策轮数窗口；其设计刻意用结构化报告替代滚动消息历史以避免「电话效应」
- quote: "Agents make decisions based solely on data available up to each trading day... TradingAgents generates trading signals to buy, sell, or hold assets... traders synthesizing insights from debates and historical data... pure natural language communication can resemble a game of telephone—over multiple iterations, initial information may be forgotten or distorted"
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20138v7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-06-03 (v7)
- confidence: high

### [11] TradingAgents 风控团队职责显式包含「设止损、调整仓位」等管理动作，三视角（激进/中性/保守）经 n 轮辩论后由基金经理改写交易计划——单笔仓位管理是多 agent 决策的一部分而非一次性事件
- quote: "Implementing risk mitigation strategies, such as setting stop-loss orders or diversifying holdings. ... Providing feedback to Trader Agents on risk exposures and suggesting adjustments to trading strategies. ... They engage in n rounds of natural language discussion, guided by a facilitator agent. ... The fund manager reviews the discussion from the risk management team, determines the appropriate risk adjustments, and updates the trader's decision"
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20138v7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-06-03 (v7)
- confidence: high
