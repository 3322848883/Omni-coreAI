# F1: AI交易系统全功能清单

## Findings

### [1] TradingAgents（arXiv:2412.20138, 2024-12/2025-06 v7）把交易组织建模为 7 类角色 + 基金经理审批：Fundamentals/Sentiment/News/Technical 四分析师、Bull/Bear 研究员辩论、带风险偏好的 Trader、三视角（risk-seeking/neutral/risk-conservative）风险管理团队，最后 Fund Manager 审批执行。
- quote: "TradingAgents defines seven distinct agent roles within a simulated trading firm: Fundamentals Analyst, Sentiment Analyst, News Analyst, Technical Analyst, Researcher, Trader, and Risk Manager"
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20138v7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-28 (v1), 2025-06-03 (v7)
- confidence: high

### [2] TradingAgents 用「结构化文档/全局状态」替代纯自然语言消息历史，只在研究员辩论和风险团队内部用 NL 对话，由 facilitator 裁决后写回结构化条目——目的是消除 telephone effect 与长上下文信息损耗。
- quote: "our framework combines structured outputs for control, clarity, and reasoning with natural language dialogue to facilitate effective debate and collaboration among agents"
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20138v7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-06-03
- confidence: high

### [3] TradingAgents 按任务复杂度混用「快思考」（gpt-4o-mini/4o 做摘要取数）与「深思考」（o1-preview 做决策报告）模型，且无需 GPU 只靠 API credits，骨干模型可插拔替换。
- quote: "Quick-thinking models, such as gpt-4o-mini and gpt-4o, efficiently handle fast, low-depth tasks... deep-thinking models like o1-preview excel in reasoning-intensive tasks"
- url: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20138v7
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-06-03
- confidence: high

### [4] FinAgent（arXiv:2402.18485, 2024-02/06）是多模态基础 agent：market intelligence 同时处理数值/文本/视觉（K 线图），配 tool augmentation、dual-level reflection、diversified memory retrieval；在含 Crypto 的 6 个数据集上对 9 个 SOTA 基线平均利润提升 36%+，单数据集最高 92.27% 收益。
- quote: "FinAgent's market intelligence module processes a diverse range of data-numerical, textual, and visual... Its unique dual-level reflection module not only enables rapid adaptation to market dynamics but also incorporates a diversified memory retrieval system"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18485
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-02-28 (v1), 2024-06-28 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [5] FinMem（arXiv:2311.13743）把 LLM 交易 agent 拆成三模块：Profiling（人设定制）、分层 Memory（layered message processing，可调 cognitive span 保留超人类感知的信息）、Decision-making；记忆对齐人类交易员认知结构以获得可解释性与实时调参。
- quote: "It encompasses three core modules: Profiling, to customize the agent's characteristics; Memory, with layered message processing... and Decision-making, to convert insights gained from memories into investment decisions"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13743
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-11-23 (v1), 2023-12-03 (v2)
- confidence: high

### [6] FinCon（arXiv:2407.06567, 2024-07/11）引入「概念化言语强化学习」：risk-control 组件周期性自我批判，把经验提炼成 systematic investment beliefs，并选择性传播到需要知识更新的节点，替代昂贵的 peer-to-peer 通信；组织结构是 manager-analyst 层级，泛化到单股交易与组合管理。
- quote: "a risk-control component in FinCon enhances decision quality by episodically initiating a self-critiquing mechanism to update systematic investment beliefs. The conceptualized beliefs serve as verbal reinforcement for the future agent's behavior"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06567
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09 (v1), 2024-11-07 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [7] FreqAI/Freqtrade 开源栈的功能远超「模型+下单」：self-adaptive retraining（live 后台线程持续重训）、10k+ 特征工程、outlier removal、PCA 降维、崩溃恢复（模型落盘+清理过期文件）、producer/consumer 机群（一机训练多机消费信号）、RL 支持、带重训模拟的回测。
- quote: "Features include: Self-adaptive retraining... Rapid feature engineering... Crash resilience... Deploying bot fleets - Set one bot to train models while a fleet of consumers use signals"
- url: https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/freqai/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live docs, cited JOSS 2022)
- confidence: high

### [8] Freqtrade 回测输出完整的绩效归因与统计检验：CAGR、Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar（closed trades 与 wallet 两套）、SQN、mean profit p-value（单样本 t 检验判断 edge 是否噪声）、profit factor、expectancy、enter-tag/exit-reason/mixed 分解、日/周/月/年 breakdown、lookahead/recursive analysis、多策略对比、手续费可覆盖。
- quote: "Mean profit p-value: Two-sided p-value of a one-sample Student's t-test against the null hypothesis that the mean per-trade return is zero... a small value... means the observed edge is unlikely to be down to chance"
- url: https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/backtesting/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live docs)
- confidence: high

### [9] Hummingbot（v2.17.0, 2026）已从纯做市框架扩成 agentic 交易栈：Condor「AI harness for building and running agentic strategies」、Hummingbot Client（318 connectors / $47B+ 报告成交量 / 111K+ 实例）、Gateway（DEX 上链）、集中 API server，并有 Reporting 实时看板按交易所/版本过滤成交量。
- quote: "Hummingbot v2.17.0 is here... AI Harness — Condor... 318 Connectors... $47B+ Total Trade Volume"
- url: https://hummingbot.org/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-22 (site date)
- confidence: high

### [10] 3Commas 商业功能矩阵（Starter $20 / Pro $50 / Expert $140 月）覆盖：DCA/GRID/Signal/Arbitrage 多类 bot、SmartTrade（并发 TP+SL、trailing TP/SL、多目标分批卖出、Smart Cover 高抛低吸）、TradingView 与第三方信号 marketplace、组合再平衡、交易日志、回测、Pine Script 执行、移动 App、开发者 API——这些是「纯 LLM 策略+执行」普遍缺失的执行与运营层。
- quote: "Concurrent Take Profit and Stop Loss... Take Profit and Stop Loss Trailing feature... Sell at multiple targets... Smart Cover... Portfolios... Marketplace... Trading Journal"
- url: https://3commas.io/features
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live site © 2026)
- confidence: high

### [11] Bitsgap（17 所统一终端，800k+ 用户，$9.46B AUM 声称）提供 7 种策略 bot（GRID/DCA/BTD/LOOP/DCA Futures/COMBO/QFL）、AI Assistant（按投资额与风险偏好给 bot 建议 + Smart Take Profit + 最优组合）、Demo 模拟盘、回测与风控工具、组合 ROI/历史导出——策略类型与 AI 辅助配置是执行层之外的另一缺口维度。
- quote: "6 crypto trading bots for beginners and PROs... AI Assistant: Get tailored bot insights based on investment amounts and risk preference... Demo mode where you trade in live market simulation"
- url: https://bitsgap.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live site 2026)
- confidence: high

### [12] 相比「纯 LLM 策略+执行」，2024-2026 系统普遍多出六层能力：(a) 组织级审批/多视角风控（TradingAgents Fund Manager + Risk Team）；(b) 自我批判与信念更新的言语强化（FinCon）；(c) 多模态+分层/可检索记忆（FinAgent/FinMem）；(d) 在线自适应重训与机群部署（FreqAI）；(e) 统计显著性回测与参数优化（Freqtrade hyperopt/p-value）；(f) 执行微结构与运营层（trailing/多目标/DCA-GRID/组合/日志/移动端/信号市场，3Commas/Bitsgap/Hummingbot）。
- quote: "opportunities to enhance multi-sourced information synthesis and optimize decision-making outcomes through timely experience refinement remain unexplored"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06567
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: medium
