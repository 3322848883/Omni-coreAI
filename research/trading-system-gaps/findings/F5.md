### [2] 策略上线前必须用 IS→WFA→OOS 三段协议锁参验证，并在时间序上用 performance decay + 回撤行为检测过拟合；配套 cliff veto / spread&leverage guard / circuit breaker / kill switch 的 defense-in-depth
- quote: "A case study on USDJPY M5 intraday data demonstrates how to detect overfitting through performance decay and drawdown behavior across chronological stages. ... ### [3] Alpha 衰减可归结为三条典型路径；拥挤衰减表现为「信号与未来收益相关性仍在但幅度显著下降 + 信号日换手异常放大」，是最难防的一类，需专门监控

### [4] 策略失效的宏观机制是「被发现→被复制→资金涌入吃掉机会→反向交易者做对手盘→alpha 被套利掉」，叠加市场结构变迁；实盘四个月击穿历史最大回撤是常见衰减信号

### [6] 策略对比应做多变体目标敏感性检验：AlgoXpert 对 alpha v1–v4 做 post-validation，目标从「最大化 Sharpe」改为「最小化最大回撤」时出现 rank reversal，说明单一指标 A/B 会误判
- quote: "A post validation comparison of four alpha variants (v1 to v4) shows rank reversal when the objective changes from maximizing Sharpe to minimizing maximum drawdown, highlighting the trade off between risk adjusted performance and tail risk control."
