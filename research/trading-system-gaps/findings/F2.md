# F2: 策略回测与过拟合检测

## Findings

### [1] LLM 策略无法直接回测 prompt，必须将 prompt 固化为可执行代码/信号后在 point-in-time 特征空间上做 lag-one 或滚动 OOS 回测；结构化 TimeGate / registry-validated tools 比事后统计校正更关键
- quote: "the agent can only act through registry-validated tools whose feature space excludes look-ahead by construction; we show that this guardrail is not redundant with statistical correction: a deliberately leaky oracle posting a Sharpe ratio of 35 survives Deflated Sharpe and probability-of-backtest-overfitting testing completely."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27734
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-27
- confidence: high

### [2] 回测 LLM 策略的主流做法是：LLM 生成策略代码/信号 → 沙箱执行 → 历史点位数据回测；FINSABER 用 20 年、100+ 标的系统回测表明窄窗口/小样本会严重夸大 LLM 策略优势
- quote: "most evaluations of LLM timing-based investing strategies are conducted on narrow timeframes and limited stock universes, overstating effectiveness due to survivorship and data-snooping biases. ... Systematic backtests over two decades and 100+ symbols reveal that previously reported LLM advantages deteriorate significantly under broader cross-section and over a longer-term evaluation."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07078
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-05-11 (v6: 2026-06-26, KDD 2026)
- confidence: high

### [3] 过拟合检测的标准统计工具是 Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) + Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO) + Superior Predictive Ability (SPA) + Minimum Track Record Length (MinTRL) 组合；但纯统计校正无法捕捉 look-ahead 泄漏
- quote: "The score combines four established validation quantities: Deflated Sharpe Ratio, Probability of Backtest Overfitting, Superior Predictive Ability, and Minimum Track Record Length, with a regime-stability diagnostic."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23808
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-24 (v2: 2026-08-26)
- confidence: high

### [4] 多重假设检验/搜索次数必须按 trial count 做 deflation：系统应记录每次策略评估，用 agent 自身搜索强度抬高显著性阈值，否则 best-of-N 选择偏差会伪造 alpha
- quote: "the system records every strategy evaluation its search performs and deflates all reported performance by that trial count, tracing how the best in-sample Sharpe ratio climbs with each trial while the deflation threshold, driven by the agent's own search, climbs faster."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27734
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-27
- confidence: high

### [5] 策略评分框架示例 MinervaScore：0-100 分 + Robustness Seal（≥80 且五门全过才显示）；合成真值 AUROC=0.989，但真实市场预注册前瞻关系不显著（Spearman ρs=0.013, p=0.40），说明评分只度量统计支持而非未来盈利概率
- quote: "In synthetic markets with known ground truth, the MinervaScore separates true signal from lucky backtest outcomes, with an AUROC of 0.989 at the headline difficulty. ... In a pre-registered test on unseen real-market data, the score showed no significant forward relationship in a population with limited surviving edge (Spearman rho_s = 0.013, one-sided permutation p = 0.40)."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23808
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-24
- confidence: high

### [6] 基准对比必须包含 buy-and-hold 与传统规则策略；FIDES 显示 40 个 LLM 策略中仅 2 个跑赢 buy-and-hold，且 sma(50,200) 的平均 Sharpe 高于所有模型；32/40 策略自我声称跑赢但只有 1 个真正跑赢
- quote: "only 2 of 40 strategies beat buy-and-hold, and a plain sma(50,200) rule outperforms every model's mean Sharpe. Second, self-assessment is badly calibrated: 32 of 40 strategies claim to beat buy-and-hold and exactly one does."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23308
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-24
- confidence: high

### [7] LLM 策略评估应做过程诊断而非收益率排名；CLQT 用五轴能力记分卡（Coherence, Acuity, Composure, Discipline, Reliability）+ 硬 TimeGate + 成本模型，并发现 capability leader ≠ Sharpe leader，agents 分配系统性偏离其自身分析（stating-vs-doing gap: 回测 +0.30 / 实盘 +0.23）
- quote: "From the audit trail CLQT computes a five-axis capability scorecard (Coherence, Acuity, Composure, Discipline, Reliability) ... the capability leader is not the Sharpe leader; that agents' allocations systematically fail to follow their own stated analysis, a stating-versus-doing gap stable across both tracks (+0.30 backtest, +0.23 live)"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29771
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-29 (v3: 2026-09-12)
- confidence: high

### [8] 训练数据泄漏/记忆污染的检测手段：(a) contamination-controlled 回测战役 + post-cutoff 实盘 paper-trading；(b) 注入 Close.shift(-1) 等未来信息探针检验 do-to-real gap；(c) 静态基准自带污染风险需用可执行验证问题过滤
- quote: "We validate CLQT on a contamination-controlled, year-long multi-model backtest campaign ... and a four-week live broker paper-trading track on post-cutoff data."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29771
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-29
- confidence: high

### [9] 未来信息注入探针可量化泄漏：注入 Close.shift(-1) 使 do-to-real 一致性下降 0.33，但运行时 future-information probe 对干净/注入代码均未触发——仅靠静态代码审查不够
- quote: "Injecting Close.shift(-1) drops do to real by 0.33 on average, while our runtime future-information probe fired on neither clean nor injected code."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23308
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-24
- confidence: high

### [10] 静态 benchmark 有数据污染风险；Backtrader-Bench 用自生成 MCQ + 可执行验证 + generator-solver 过滤（丢弃无工具可答问题）来规避污染；工具增强 agent 单次 90.0% vs 无工具最优 73.0%，另采 38 题上无工具掉到约随机 25%
- quote: "Evaluating LLM coding agents in algorithmic trading is difficult because static benchmarks risk data contamination and numerical backtest outputs require ground truth from actual code execution. ... Tool-augmented agents reach 90.0% accuracy in a single pass (GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.7), outperforming the best no-tools baselines (73.0%, averaged over 10 runs) by 17 percentage points."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11232
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-07-30 (IJCAI 2026 FinLLM Workshop)
- confidence: high

### [11] Walk-forward 验证标准协议：IS（稳定参数区而非单点最优）→ WFA（滚动窗 + purge gap 防信息泄漏 + majority pass / catastrophic veto）→ OOS（严格参数锁定禁止再调参）；performance decay 与 drawdown 行为是过拟合信号
- quote: "evaluates strategies across three stages: In Sample (IS), which focuses on stable parameter regions instead of single optima; Walk Forward Analysis (WFA) using rolling windows and purge gaps to reduce information leakage, supported by majority pass and catastrophic veto rules; and Out of Sample (OOS) testing under strict parameter lock with no further tuning."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09219
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-03-10
- confidence: high

### [12] 开源回测框架对 LLM 策略的支持是拼装式的：Backtrader 作为代码生成目标（QuantCode-Bench 400 任务、BacktestBench 18,246 QA）；FinRL-X 统一 data→strategy→backtest→broker 并支持 RL allocator + LLM 情绪信号；主流框架本身不提供 DSR/PBO/防污染能力，需外挂
- quote: "the official FinRL-X implementation is available at https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL-Trading" / "we present QuantCode-Bench, a benchmark for the systematic evaluation of modern LLMs in generating strategies for the Backtrader framework"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21330
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-03-22 (FinRL-X); QuantCode-Bench https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15151 (2026-04-16)
- confidence: medium
