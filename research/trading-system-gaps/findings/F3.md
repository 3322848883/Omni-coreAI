# F3: 智能执行与订单管理

## Findings

### [1] 加密场景下的智能路由（SOR）主要解决多所流动性碎片化，核心是实时比价/拆单到最优场所，并常以 DEX 聚合器形态落地
- quote: "SOR addresses challenges such as fragmented liquidity and price inconsistencies across numerous exchanges and decentralized platforms. SOR systems in crypto often function as decentralized exchange (DEX) aggregators. They scan multiple decentralized exchanges to find optimal trade routes that offer the best prices and liquidity."
- url: https://www.coinapi.io/learn/glossary/smart-order-routing-sor
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [2] 2024 年机构级 SOR 已覆盖加密/数字资产，且开始引入 ML/AI 做预测性路由与动态策略调整
- quote: "multiple asset classes, including crypto and digital assets. Today, machine learning and artificial intelligence are steadily being integrated to improve SOR performance through predictive analytics, adaptive learning, and dynamic strategy adjustments."
- url: https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/the-top-12-smart-order-routing-technologies-in-2024/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2024
- confidence: medium

### [3] 加密市场 VWAP 执行应直接优化执行目标而非预测成交量曲线，直接优化在波动环境下滑点更低
- quote: "Traditional approaches typically focus on forecasting the market's volume curve, an assumption that may hold true under steady conditions but becomes suboptimal in more volatile environments or markets such as cryptocurrency where prediction error margins are higher. ... my method calibrates order allocation to minimize VWAP slippage"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13722
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-02-19
- confidence: high

### [4] Hyperliquid 上公开可见的协议原生 TWAP 订单（sunshine trading）执行成本更低、永久冲击更小，且会吸引深度挂单
- quote: "Visible TWAPs face lower execution costs than comparable hidden metaorders and leave a smaller permanent price impact. ... while active, displayed depth rises and the book tilts toward the absorbing side"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15715
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-14
- confidence: high

### [5] 订单状态机需区分 live / partially_filled / filled / canceled / mmp_canceled，部分成交后的 IOC 会带非零 accFillSz 进入终态
- quote: "live : on the order book, no fills yet. partially_filled : partially executed, still active on book. filled : fully executed, terminal state. canceled : cancelled, terminal state. For IOC orders partially filled before cancellation, accFillSz may be non-zero. mmp_canceled : automatically cancelled by Market Maker Protection, terminal state."
- url: https://www.okx.com/docs-v5/en/#order-book-trading-trade-get-order-details
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (current OKX v5 docs)
- confidence: high

### [6] 交易所 API 限频按维度区分（IP / UserID / 连接），超限返回明确错误码；OKX 还按成交率动态上调子账户配额
- quote: "When a request is rejected by our system due to rate limits, the system returns error code 50011 ... Public unauthenticated REST rate limits are based on IP address. Private REST rate limits are based on User ID ... the exchange will offer a higher sub-account rate limit to clients with a high trade fill ratio."
- url: https://www.okx.com/docs-v5/en/#overview-rate-limits
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (current OKX v5 docs)
- confidence: high

### [7] 交易所原生 TWAP 算法单有数量上限（OKX 每合约 20 笔挂起），市价单可配 slippagePct 做硬滑点保护
- quote: "The maximum number of pending algo orders: ... TWAP order: 20 ... slippagePct String No Maximum acceptable slippage for spot and spot margin market-side orders ... Range: 0 to 0.05 (0% to 5%, inclusive). ... Slippage cannot be modified on an existing order. Cancel and resubmit to change the slippage setting."
- url: https://www.okx.com/docs-v5/en/#order-book-trading-trade-post-place-order
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (current OKX v5 docs)
- confidence: high

### [8] Flashbots Protect 通过私有 mempool 隐藏交易以防抢跑/夹子，并保证失败交易不上链、不付费
- quote: "Frontrunning protection: Transactions are hidden from the public mempool away from front-running and sandwich bots. ... No failed transactions: Transactions are only included in the block if they do not revert. Users do not pay fees for failed transactions."
- url: https://docs.flashbots.net/flashbots-protect/quick-start
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (current Flashbots docs)
- confidence: high

### [9] UniswapX 用 intent + 竞争性拍卖（如 Dutch Auction）跨公私流动性源路由，提供 MEV 保护且失败零成本
- quote: "UniswapX's intent-based infrastructure uses competitive auctions to route swaps across public and private liquidity sources, all gasless with no cost for failed swaps. ... Protection against MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)"
- url: https://docs.uniswap.org/contracts/uniswapx/overview
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (current Uniswap docs)
- confidence: high

### [10] 被夹后用户行为会迁移：约 40% 在 60 天内转向私有路由，重复被夹升至 54%；但私有通道仍可被夹，需协议级防护
- quote: "around 40% of victims migrate to private routing within 60 days, rising to 54% with repeated exposures. ... private routing does not guarantee protection from MEV extraction: while execution failures push users toward private channels, these remain exploitable and highly concentrated"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17602
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-12-19
- confidence: high

### [11] DEX 滑点应拆成良性与对抗性（reordering）两部分；大额单以价格冲击+滑点为主，memecoin 对抗性滑点概率约高 80%
- quote: "our main tool is slippage -- the difference between the realized execution price of a trade, and its quoted price -- which we breakdown into its benign and adversarial components. ... for large swaps price-impact and slippage account for the majority of it. Moreover, when trading PEPE ... the probability of adversarial slippage is about 80% higher"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.13648
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-09-24 (rev 2024-04-17)
- confidence: high

### [12] 将 orderbook imbalance（OBI）嵌入拆单执行算法可在多数市况下改善执行；大单直接暴露会招致抢跑与冲击
- quote: "Order book imbalance (OBI) - buy orders minus sell orders near the best quote - measures supply-demand imbalance that can move prices. ... Large orders placed at once can reveal intent, invite front-running, raise volatility, and cause losses. Execution algorithms therefore split parent orders into smaller lots to limit price distortion."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16912
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-21
- confidence: high

### [13] RL 最优执行框架把市价/限价分配建模为动态决策，在含噪声者、战术者（响应 OBI）的 LOB 环境中优于传统基准策略
- quote: "We formulate the trade execution problem as a dynamic allocation task whose objective is the optimal placement of market and limit orders to maximize expected revenue. ... the proposed method outperforms traditional benchmark strategies in simulated limit order book environments featuring noise traders ... tactical traders responding to order book imbalances"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06345
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-07-08 (rev 2026-01-26)
- confidence: high
