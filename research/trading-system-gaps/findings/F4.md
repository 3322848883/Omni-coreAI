# F4: 组合风控与仓位管理

## Findings

### [1] LLM 风控指令不能停留在自然语言描述，必须转换为可执行的类型化约束并在成交组合上强制校验
- quote: "Natural-language risk mandates are converted into typed constraints and enforced on the executed portfolio. Each run produces audit artifacts, including a contamination certificate, a cost-sensitivity curve, and a constraint-adherence report."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09988
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-06
- confidence: high

### [2] 成熟 LLM 交易框架用「构造器 LLM 提权重 + 确定性 critic 保可行」的分层架构，把可行性从模型自觉中剥离
- quote: "a constructor LLM proposes weights, and a deterministic critic guarantees feasibility"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09988
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-06
- confidence: high

### [3] 多 agent 自主交易管线应含独立 Risk Manager 代理 + 确定性硬门安全层，而非仅靠执行前检查
- quote: "an Analyst agent, a Risk Manager agent, and an Executor agent form a structured reasoning chain governed by typed JSON contracts and a deterministic hard-gate safety layer"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12532
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-05-01
- confidence: high

### [4] 组合级相关性/集中度控制可操作化为 Correlation-Break Diversification 分数，在单笔信号推理中优先低相关、特质性机会
- quote: "a Correlation-Break Diversification composite score that operationalizes portfolio-level idiosyncratic signal prioritization within individual agent reasoning"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12532
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-05-01
- confidence: high

### [5] FinCon 用周期性 self-critique 的 risk-control 组件更新系统投资信念，是 LLM 交易风控的经验闭环设计
- quote: "a risk-control component in FinCon enhances decision quality by episodically initiating a self-critiquing mechanism to update systematic investment beliefs"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06567
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [6] 组合管理抽象应从单资产动作扩展到多资产配置，整合风险管理与跨资产推理；输出应是风险收益平衡的百分比配置
- quote: "a portfolio-management abstraction that extends control from single-asset actions to multi-asset allocation, integrating risk management and cross-asset reasoning"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03628
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-11-05
- confidence: high

### [7] 波动率目标（vol targeting）是最简单有效的组合级仓位缩放：按历史波动动态混入现金以锁定目标波动，20 年样本上同时改善风险调整收益与回撤
- quote: "all risk-adjusted and drawdown metrics are improved using simple volatility control, where we dynamically mix the fixed-weight portfolios with cash so as to target a fixed volatility"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07946
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-07
- confidence: high

### [8] 波动率目标在可比资产上可优于 risk parity / minimum variance；凸优化组合（类对冲基金）还能再改进
- quote: "These portfolios also outperform a suite of standard risk-based allocation methods, such as risk parity and minimum variance, evaluated on the same assets and data."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07946
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-07
- confidence: high

### [9] 仓位管理上 Kelly 与波动率 regime 缩放的混合方法比单一 Kelly 更能兼顾收益与回撤控制（2025 实证）
- quote: "The hybrid sizing method consistently balances return generation with robust drawdown control, particularly under low-volatility conditions such as those seen in 2024."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16598
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-08-09
- confidence: high

### [10] 崩盘风险意识可改进 Kelly 仓位：知道崩盘幅度（即使不知时点）也能带来稳健优势，且对期望收益误差最敏感
- quote: "knowledge of only the size, and not the time of occurrence, already provides a significant and robust edge. ... The method is most sensitive to errors in the expected return."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09368
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-04-20
- confidence: medium

### [11] 尾部/回撤硬约束可用 control barrier functions 在动作执行层强制 drawdown limits，而非事后止损
- quote: "BRaG incorporates control barrier functions that constrain action execution and regularize policy learning to satisfy drawdown limits."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15770
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-16
- confidence: medium

### [12] LLM 策略需外部「Strict Mode」风险锚：严格约束在下跌市中抑制回撤；无约束模型易陷入 high-win-rate trap（小赚大亏）
- quote: "standard models require Strict Mode as a vital risk anchor; ... Strict Mode mitigates drawdowns in downtrends; ... standard LLMs often fall into a high win-rate trap, optimizing for small gains at the expense of total returns, which can only be mitigated through deep reasoning or strict external guardrails."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06024
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-05-07
- confidence: high
