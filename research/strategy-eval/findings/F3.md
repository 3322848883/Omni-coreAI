# F3: Position sizing formulas, heat/portfolio limits, strategy correlation, drawdown circuit breakers, prop-firm daily-loss/max-DD de-facto standards

## Findings

### [1] FTMO 2-Step CFD de-facto standard is 5% Maximum Daily Loss and 10% static Maximum Loss of Initial Simulated Capital
- quote: "The Maximum Daily Loss Amount, which is 5% of the Initial Simulated Capital" / "the Maximum Loss Amount, which is 10% of the Initial Simulated Capital"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [2] FTMO 1-Step CFD tightens daily loss to 3% and uses end-of-day trailing 10% Maximum Loss plus a Best Day Rule of 50%
- quote: "the Maximum Daily Loss Amount, which is 3% of the Initial Simulated Capital" / "the Best Day Rule requires that your Best Day does not represent more than 50% of your Positive Days' Profit"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [3] FTMO Futures uses EOD-trailing Max Drawdown of $2,000 on $50k, $3,500 on $100k, $5,000 on $150k and locks permanently at Initial Simulated Capital
- quote: "The Maximum Drawdown Limit is calculated at the start of each trading day as the difference between the highest account balance recorded at the close of any preceding trading day (or the Initial Simulated Capital if higher) and the applicable Maximum Drawdown Amount. ... Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital, it locks permanently at that figure"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/futures/trading-objectives-and-rules/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [4] FTMO Futures Daily Loss Limits are $1,000 on $50k, $1,500 on $100k, $2,000 on $150k (~2% / 1.5% / 1.33% of starting capital) and re-anchor to each day's start balance
- quote: "The Daily Loss Limit Amount is $1,000. ... the Daily Loss Limit decreases as well. Unlike the Maximum Drawdown Limit, it can both increase and decrease."
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/futures/trading-objectives-and-rules/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [5] FTMO Futures Consistency Rule caps Best Day at 40% (Growth) or 50% (Pro) of total Evaluation profit; breach is not fatal but blocks pass until more profit is accumulated
- quote: "the Consistency Rule requires that your single most profitable trading day (the Best Day) does not represent more than 40% of your total profit" (Growth); "more than 50% of your total profit" (Pro)
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/futures/trading-objectives-and-rules/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [6] FTMO Futures hard-caps concurrent contracts at 5 / 10 / 15 standard-or-mini contracts for 50k / 100k / 150k accounts (10 micros = 1 mini)
- quote: "The Max Contracts limit on your Evaluation Account is 5 contracts: One Standard or Mini contract counts as one contract; Ten Micro contracts count as one Standard / Mini contract"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/futures/trading-objectives-and-rules/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [7] FTMO's own risk guidance for professionals is ~1% account risk per trade, not leverage-max
- quote: "Remember that professionals usually risk only 1% and still make very interesting profits in a safe and stable environment and long term."
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/blog/gambling-is-not-the-right-way-to-make-money/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-08-05 (modified 2025-11-04)
- confidence: high

### [8] Practical Kelly sizing for discrete P&L is f = kelly_fraction × (p − q/b); full Kelly is treated as too volatile and fractional Kelly (commonly 0.5) is the default
- quote: "Kelly 需要胜率与盈亏比;f = kelly_fraction × (p − q/b)" / "KellySizer —— 分数 Kelly,满 Kelly 太颠簸,默认取半" (kelly_fraction=0.5)
- url: https://github.com/SilentFleetKK/riskguard
- source_type: community
- published: unknown (repo active 2026)
- confidence: medium

### [9] Open-source risk layers standardize on ~10% single-name position cap and a 15% drawdown circuit breaker that blocks new risk but always allows risk-reducing orders
- quote: "MaxPositionLimit — 任一标的名义敞口 ≤ 权益的 max_position_pct(默认 10%)" / "DrawdownCircuitBreaker — 回撤触及 max_drawdown_pct(默认 15%)即停新仓,减仓永远放行"
- url: https://github.com/SilentFleetKK/riskguard
- source_type: community
- published: unknown (repo active 2026)
- confidence: medium

### [10] Deterministic simulation shows 10% position cap + 15% drawdown breaker cut max drawdown from −45.0% to −5.6% on a −45% crash (same signals), at the cost of capturing only ~10% of a +200% bull
- quote: "同一段崩盘,风控把最大回撤从 45% 压到 5.6%" / "风控账户只吃到裸奔涨幅的 10% —— 120,000 vs 300,000"
- url: https://github.com/SilentFleetKK/riskguard
- source_type: community
- published: unknown (examples/06_with_vs_without.py, examples/09)

### [11] riskkit encodes a tiered drawdown ladder (3% / 5% / 7% size cuts, 10% halt) and anti-martingale sizing with base_risk 1%, hard max_risk 1.5%, max_notional 4% of equity
- quote: "PositionSizer ... base_risk_pct=1.0, max_risk_pct=1.5, max_notional_pct=4.0" / "DrawdownManager(tier1_pct=3, tier2_pct=5, tier3_pct=7, halt_pct=10)" / "Anti-martingale. Size goes down after losses and during drawdowns, never up."
- url: https://github.com/HasibVortex369/riskkit

### [12] Portfolio heat is defined as total risk-at-stop across open positions, and CorrelationGuard allows at most one open position per correlation group (static or rolling return-correlation)
- quote: "Portfolio caps ... total open notional, total heat (risk-at-stop), and per-sector / asset-class exposure" / "CorrelationGuard: At most one open position per correlation group. Groups can be static ... or computed dynamically from a rolling return-correlation matrix."
- url: https://github.com/HasibVortex369/riskkit
- source_type: community
- published: unknown (2026)
- confidence: medium

- quote: "Position sizing — the largest position that cannot breach the account, sized against the drawdown floor rather than the balance." / "For trailing accounts it follows your high-water mark up — until it locks at your starting balance (the Topstep/Apex behavior)"
- url: https://github.com/shootingallday/propfirm-calc

- published: unknown (updated 2026-09-23)
