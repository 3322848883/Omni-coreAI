# F4: Strategy leaderboards/scoreboards, paper-to-live promotion checklists, and observability/audit for LLM/automated trading

## Findings

### [1] FTMO 2-Step evaluation gates promotion on concurrent Profit Target (10% Challenge / 5% Verification), Maximum Daily Loss 5% of initial capital, Maximum Loss 10% static, and at least 4 Minimum Trading Days.
- quote: "The Profit Target is calculated as a percentage of your Initial Simulated Capital: 10% for the FTMO Challenge; 5% for the Verification"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [2] FTMO 1-Step replaces two-phase evaluation with a single pass but adds a Best Day Rule requiring the single best day to be ≤50% of Positive Days' Profit (soft gate: excess does not breach, but raises the required total profit).
- quote: "the Best Day Rule requires that your Best Day does not represent more than 50% of your Positive Days' Profit"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [3] FTMO 1-Step risk envelope: Maximum Daily Loss 3% of Initial Simulated Capital (recalculated at 00:00 CE(S)T vs prior day balance) and Maximum Loss 10% end-of-day trailing (can only increase), with rule continued on the funded FTMO Account.
- quote: "the Maximum Daily Loss Amount, which is 3% of the Initial Simulated Capital"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-24
- confidence: high

### [4] Topstep Trading Combine is a one-rule / two-objective scoreboard: do not breach Maximum Loss Limit; achieve Profit Target ($3k/$6k/$9k on $50K/$100K/$150K); keep best day below 55% of Profit Target — minimum 2 trading days, cannot pass in one day.
- quote: "It's a simulated account with one rule and two objectives. Meet them, and you've earned your Express Funded Account"
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284197-trading-combine-parameters
- source_type: primary
- published: "updated this week (as of 2026-09-28)"
- confidence: high

### [5] Topstep Consistency Target uses the formula Best Day Profit ÷ Total Profit, with a hard 55% line in Combine (exceeding raises the Profit Target via Best Day ÷ 0.55) and a stricter 40% Consistency Objective for XFA payout eligibility.
- quote: "Your single best day of profit must stay at or below 55% of your Profit Target. If it exceeds that, your Profit Target increases."
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284208-consistency-at-topstep
- source_type: primary
- published: "updated over a week ago (as of 2026-09-28)"
- confidence: high

### [6] Topstep's paper-to-live ladder is Combine (sim) → Express Funded Account (sim, real payouts) → Live Funded Account (real capital); Live call-up is discretionary by the Risk Team based on tape/consistency, not a formulaic threshold, and declining means closing the XFA.
- quote: "That's the goal — the call-up. The Risk Team is watching your tape. Keep trading consistently and let your performance make the case."
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284215-express-funded-account-parameters
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-05
- confidence: high

### [7] Topstep XFA payout gates are metricized: Standard path requires 5 winning days ≥$150 each; Consistency path requires 3 trading days and Consistency ≤40%; max payout 50% of balance up to $5K–$6K with 90/10 profit split; consistency resets after each Payout.
- quote: "Payout eligibility: 5 winning days of $150+ / 3 days traded, 40% consistency target"
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284215-express-funded-account-parameters
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-05
- confidence: high

### [8] Topstep Live capital is staged for paper-to-live risk control: only 20% of transferred balance (min $10,000) is immediately tradable; 80% sits in Reserve released in four 25% increments at $3K/$6K/$9K profit thresholds; balance <$1,000 triggers liquidation and account close.
- quote: "20% available to trade immediately — minimum $10,000; 80% held in Reserve — released in 4 increments of 25% as you hit profit thresholds"
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/10657969-live-funded-account-parameters
- source_type: primary
- published: "updated over a week ago (as of 2026-09-28)"
- confidence: high

### [9] Topstep Live scales risk controls down automatically as equity falls: Daily Loss Limit $2,000/$3,000/$4,500 on $50K/$100K/$150K, then DLL and max position size auto-tighten at tradable balance ≤$10,000 (DLL $2,000, max 5 contracts) and ≤$5,000 (DLL $1,000, max 3 contracts).
- quote: "if your tradable balance drops to lower thresholds, the DLL and Maximum Position Size automatically adjust"
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/10657969-live-funded-account-parameters
- source_type: primary
- published: "updated over a week ago (as of 2026-09-28)"
- confidence: high

### [10] FTMO Scaling Plan grows account size 25% every 4 months (up to $2,000,000 aggregate) only if ≥10% net simulated profit in the prior 4 months, ≥2 processed Rewards in that window, ≥4 months tenure, and positive balance at scale-up.
- quote: "At least 10% net simulated profit above the starting balance, generated within the prior 4 months; At least 2 processed Rewards within that same 4-month period"
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/reward-growth-and-scaling-plan/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2026-09-15
- confidence: high

### [11] QuantConnect Algorithm Lab promotes strategies via a formal Research Pipeline (research validation → backtest → paper → deployment to production) and a Strategy Explorer leaderboard sorted by OOS 3M Sharpe (out-of-sample 3-month Sharpe), not raw return.
- quote: "A structured approach to bring your ideas through research validation, backtest, paper and deployment to production trading."
- url: https://www.quantconnect.com/strategies/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live product UI, retrieved 2026-09-28)
- confidence: high

### [12] MiFID II Article 17 is the EU audit baseline for automated trading: firms must keep sufficient records for supervisory monitoring; HFT firms must store accurate time-sequenced records of all placed orders (including cancellations), executed orders and quotations, and authorities may demand strategy descriptions, trading parameters/limits, compliance controls, and testing details.
- quote: "shall store in an approved form accurate and time sequenced records of all its placed orders, including cancellations of orders, executed orders and quotations on trading venues"
- url: https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mifid-ii/article-17-algorithmic-trading
- source_type: primary
- published: Directive 2014/65/EU of 15 May 2014 (ISRB page updated 2026-07-24)
- confidence: high

### [13] MiFID II Art.17(1) also requires pre-trade risk controls (trading thresholds/limits, prevention of erroneous orders / disorderly market contribution), business continuity for system failure, full testing and continuous monitoring, plus notification of algorithmic trading to home and venue competent authorities.
- quote: "shall have in place effective systems and risk controls suitable to the business it operates to ensure that its trading systems are resilient... are subject to appropriate trading thresholds and limits and prevent the sending of erroneous orders"
- url: https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mifid-ii/article-17-algorithmic-trading
- source_type: primary
- published: Directive 2014/65/EU of 15 May 2014
- confidence: high

### [14] FARSIGHT SoK (arXiv:2609.19705, 2026-09-17) evaluated 15 academic financial LLM trading schemes and found 80% fail at least one core robustness metric (including flash-crash-like turbulence) and 100% exhibit security vulnerabilities across three attack classes: attacks on information sources, attacks on agents, and agent-as-attacker behaviors.
- quote: "Applying FARSIGHT to 15 representative academic schemes, we find that most overlook robustness and realistic adversarial threats: 80% fail at least one core robustness metric and 100% exhibit security vulnerabilities."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19705
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-17
- confidence: high

### [15] FARSIGHT frames LLM trading agents as high-consequence systems where a single compromised agent has direct execution authority over real capital in a reflexive market, so observability must cover adversarial/info-source integrity — not just P&L metrics — before paper-to-live promotion.
- quote: "a single compromised agent has direct execution authority over real capital in an adversarial, reflexive market"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19705
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-17
- confidence: high

### [16] TradingAgents (arXiv:2412.20138) is the canonical multi-persona LLM trading firm shape: fundamental/sentiment/technical analysts, Bull/Bear researchers, traders with varied risk profiles, and a risk-management team monitoring exposure; evaluation uses cumulative returns, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown.
- quote: "featuring LLM-powered agents in specialized roles such as fundamental analysts, sentiment analysts, technical analysts, and traders with varied risk profiles. The framework includes Bull and Bear researcher agents... and a risk management team monitoring exposure"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20138
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-28 (v7 updated 2025-06-03)
- confidence: high

### [17] Topstep permits automated strategies in sim stages but explicitly disclaims support for malfunctions, requires Practice-Account testing first, and blocks automated strategies on Live Funded Accounts because the API Gateway is sim-only — a concrete paper-to-live automation gap.
- quote: "The API Gateway is built for the simulated environment and isn't available on Live, so automated strategies are not possible at this time in the Live Funded Account."
- url: https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/10657969-live-funded-account-parameters
- source_type: primary
- published: "updated over a week ago (as of 2026-09-28)"
- confidence: high

### [18] FTMO's public leaderboard is a simple profitability ranking of active funded accounts ("Overview of currently most profitable active FTMO Accounts") — product shape is marketing/social proof, not a risk-adjusted scoreboard with drawdown or consistency filters.
- quote: "Overview of currently most profitable active FTMO Accounts."
- url: https://ftmo.com/en/leaderboard/
- source_type: primary
- published: dateModified 2025-09-22
- confidence: high
